## The 2 meta-principles

| Principle | Grade | Receipt | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The flywheel** — each build adds primitives; the next build is cheaper | **HAVE, demonstrated this week** | `extension/v28-files-interface.sql`'s own header names its lineage explicitly: "mirroring the ratified autonomous-materializer split (bridge-side Go goroutine, LISTEN/NOTIFY + safety poll, path-validated writes — `.spec/proposals/autonomous-materializer.md`, D-AM-1..3)". `dropwatcher.go`/`projector.go` reuse that exact LISTEN/NOTIFY-plus-poll shape rather than inventing a new one. The lightening batch (`.spec/proposals/files-as-interface-db-as-engine.md`, item 4) names the shelf-of-capabilities principle directly ("Packs: loreworks, crawler, yt-frames, brainstorm-lens-zoo, code-graph → opt-in packs"). | The *engineering discipline* of the flywheel (reuse the mechanics, cite the lineage, don't reinvent) is real and freshly proven in these two merges. The *productized* form — a formal opt-in "pack" system a deployer toggles on/off — is named as next-arc work, not yet shipped as of today. Practiced, not yet packaged. |
| **Clean-data-enables-cheap-models** | **HAVE, foundational** | `extension/v27-lifeless-core.sql` (ratified the same day as v28): "default is no models, it's just a db that's lifeless. you give it models to bring it to life" (Michael, quoted verbatim in the file header). Core seeds **zero** providers/models. v28's own header states compliance directly: "Ingest of plain markdown/text works with ZERO models configured — the docs row lands, provenance is stamped, and embedding/extraction ride the EXISTING triggers, which already degrade gracefully." | Nate frames this as a downstream *benefit* (clean data lets you reach for a cheaper model later). The substrate encodes it as a *founding constraint*: not just "cheaper model," but "no model at all" for the mechanical majority — ingest, store, structural retrieve, and the gate are all deterministic SQL/Go with zero LLM calls in the path. A stronger, earlier-enforced version of his own insight — though it was only ratified as an architecture principle the same day as the merges under review, so it's fresh, not yet battle-tested across a full deployer cycle. |

---

## Verdict

**Score: 6 of 9 primitives HAVE, 2 PARTIAL, 1 MISSING — both meta-principles HAVE, one of them (clean-data) arguably deeper than his own framing.** Call it roughly three-quarters of the skeleton genuinely present after these two merges, with the two meta-principles — the part he says most people skip — landing solid.

**Weakest single primitive: NORMALIZE.** It is the only one of the nine with no substitute at all, not even a differently-shaped one. Chunk lost its deterministic form but gained engram extraction; export lost the domain-specific packet shape but gained a general prose-projection mechanism. Normalize has nothing standing in for it — no typed date/amount extraction, no missing-document-as-first-class concept, anywhere in 28 volumes of schema. If this substrate were pointed at Nate's actual insurance-denial-letter case tomorrow, it would ingest the PDF, pool it as a doc, hybrid-search it, cite it correctly, and gate any resulting appeal letter behind Hinge review and spend caps — but nothing would reliably pull "deadline: 2026-08-15" out into a queryable, typed field the way his skeleton promises. That gap is real, and it sits directly upstream of EXPORT's weakness too: you cannot build a denial-map or evidence-checklist object out of prose that was never normalized into typed facts in the first place.

The runner-up gap is CHUNK, and it's the more interesting one: not an absence but a **deliberate trade**, made in council on 2026-05-15, away from cheap deterministic structural chunking toward flexible but model-costly engram compilation. That trade is defensible for the substrate's actual workload (unstructured research corpora, video transcripts, wiki prose) but it is the one place where Nate's specific thesis — "boring and normalized is what lets you drop to a cheap model" — cuts directly against a choice this substrate already made on purpose.
