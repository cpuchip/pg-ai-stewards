This is a synthetic policy excerpt for the case-file digester demo. No real insurer or policy is represented.

NOTE FOR DEMO READERS: Section 4.2(b) below deliberately CONTRADICTS what the demo denial letter claims it says. That planted mismatch is the inverse-hypothesis fixture for the `citation_check` sanity oracle — the demo is supposed to catch it as finding #1. Do not "fix" it.

## Section 4.1 — Covered services

Outpatient and inpatient services listed in the Schedule of Benefits are covered when medically necessary and delivered by a participating provider.

## Section 4.2(a) — Inpatient admissions

All non-emergency inpatient admissions require pre-authorization at least five (5) business days before admission.

## Section 4.2(b) — Outpatient pre-authorization

Pre-authorization is required only for elective, non-emergency outpatient procedures. Emergency and urgent outpatient care is exempt from pre-authorization and is instead subject to retrospective review under Section 5.2.

## Section 5.1 — Appeals

A member may appeal an adverse determination in writing within forty-five (45) days of the notice date. Appeals accompanied by a physician's letter of medical necessity receive expedited review.
